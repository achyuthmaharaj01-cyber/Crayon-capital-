# Critic rubric (strict). Every item is pass/fail. Any fail sends the scene back.

For each shot, look at the contact stills (at least 3 per shot: start, on its key word, end) and, for each transition, the 9-frame strip.

## A. Client rules
1. No hard cuts: every transition is push/match/whip/dissolve; no frame is black, grey mush or ghosted (two misaligned images).
2. Named-on-screen rule: each noun the narration names that is visible gets a camera push onto it, on that word, framed alone, with nobody cut by the edge.
3. Characters are 45–60% of frame height in character shots; no character is cut by the frame edge (fully in or fully out).
4. Subtitles never cover a face, a label the joke depends on, or a key prop.
5. Teeth rule (zoom in 3x on any grin).
6. The camera is always moving; no dead holds over 0.5 s.

## B. Style bible (§2–§6)
7. One ink weight (4.6 px); fills muted; at most one accent colour per shot; warm practical lights glow.
8. Egg-people proportions; floating head; single-line arms and legs; blank-faced extras; named characters consistent with `CAST.md` across every appearance (same colours, hat, hair, glasses).
9. Sets are dense and believable (not empty gradients); period-correct 1957–1964 (no modern objects).
10. Text: Caveat for cards and callouts, Kalam for signage; spelling of every name is correct (Winston Scott, Eduardo Díaz Silveti, Warren Dean, Robert Zambernardi, Boris Kolomyakov, Jeff Maysh, Sydney Wesley Finer, Tampico, Auditorio Nacional).

## C. Storytelling
11. Each beat lands on its word (±0.15 s); the picture says the same thing as the sentence, at that moment.
12. Hedged claims are marked visually and never shown as confirmed fact.
13. Geography and continuity are clear: who is where, the truck's route, the time of night (clock motif), the crate's state (closed, roof open, resealed).
14. There is a joke or a striking image at least every ~15 s (deadpan reaction, label gag, visual rhyme).
15. Characters show clear expressions; the acting fits the beat.

## Output
Write `critique/actN.md`: a table of FAILs (shot key, time, rubric #, what is wrong, the exact fix), then a short list of "would make it better" notes. End with a verdict: **PASS** only when there are zero FAILs.
