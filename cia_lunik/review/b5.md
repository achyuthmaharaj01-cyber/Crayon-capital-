# Critic review: b5 (D 7 A.M., L WHAT THEY LEARNED, A AFTERMATH): `web/shots_b5.js`

**Verdict: FIX REQUIRED (not shippable yet).** The structure, hedging and continuity are strong: the same crate in the night/morning split, DECLASSIFIED 1995, the Texas map. But the emotional peak (the checker) is undersold, several labels are cropped, the "5" reads as "S", the ending gets eaten by the fade-out, and one shot invents a mechanism.

Method: contact sheets `contact/critic_b5_01..07.jpg`, plus snaps at 646.2, 665.9, 676.3, 720.5, 732.5, 734.4, 739.9, 740.05, 742.6 s. Word times come from `voice/lines.json`.

## Required fixes

1. **BLOCKER: A8.2 (end card) / ending, owned by orchestrator + b5.**
   - **Problem:** "next" is spoken at 761.88 s and the timeline ends at 762.65 s. The engine's global fade-out starts at duration − 1.2 = 761.45 s, so NEXT: THE SUBMARINE is fading before the narrator even says "next". The tease never gets a clean beat, and that beat is what makes people click the next video.
   - **Change:** the orchestrator pads the timeline/audio with a 3.0 s tail after the voice (last shot `end` = voice end + 3). b5 then lands the "THAT STORY IS NEXT" reveal on 'next' and holds it fully lit for at least 2 s before the fade.

2. **MAJOR: D5.0/0–1 (the checker, the film's payoff).**
   - **Problem:** at 665.9 s the checker's eyes are closed with a smile. That reads as content or smug, not "showed no surprise". It is the deadpan comedy beat of the whole heist, and it currently plays as a cute nap.
   - **Change:** use a dead-flat face (open dot eyes, straight-line mouth, no brows) and hold it perfectly still for the whole line: no idle bob, and freeze his animation from 'showed'. The thought bubble should be an obvious EMPTY bubble, with the stray grey dot removed, or show a single small "…". Let the quote card type in sync with the words, as it does now, and push in slowly on his face.

3. **MAJOR: D8.0/0 and D8.0/1.**
   - **Problem:** the checker grins while signing, which breaks the deadpan established in D5. The signature is a tiny "∿" squiggle with no flourish, under-delivering the "signed it back in" sting.
   - **Change:**
     - Keep `expr: 'neutral'` (flat) throughout D4–D8.
     - Draw the signature as an animated pen stroke across the line on 'signed' (676.12), with a `stamp` or `pencil` sfx.
     - In D8.0/1 he turns away on 'without a second look' (677.04) while the train leaves. Do not show him glancing back.

4. **MAJOR: A2.0 (truth rule).**
   - **Problem:** Kolomyakov holds a photo of the rocket/crate. That invents both who found out (the script says "they", not Kolomyakov) and how (photos). The script explicitly says no one has established how or when.
   - **Change:** no named person, no evidence prop. Use a generic Soviet silhouette, or a closed Soviet file labelled SOVIET SIDE, with a large "?" and the tag PER MAYSH: MAY HAVE FOUND OUT LATER.
   - **Also:** the tag is cropped by the top edge at 720.5 s. Move it to y ≥ 120 so it sits fully in frame.

5. **MAJOR: L5.0/1 and L6.1 ("5" reads as "S").**
   - **Problem:** in Kalam the digit 5 inside the magnifier, the caption "THE 5TH EVER MADE" and the conveyor tag "#5" all read as "S" ("THE STH", "#S"). This is the chapter's key fact and it is illegible.
   - **Change:** use a font with an unambiguous 5 for numerals, or draw the digit as strokes (a flat top bar, vertical, bowl). Write the caption as "No. 5 OF ITS KIND" or "THE FIFTH EVER MADE".

6. **MAJOR: L3.0/1 and L4.0 (cropped label boxes).**
   - **Problem:** the "LUNIK / ELECTRICAL – SUPPLIERS NAMED" and "LUNIK / SYSTEM – IN THIS VEHICLE" title boxes are cut off at the right frame edge ("LUNIK / ELECTR", "SUPPLIERS NAMEI").
   - **Change:** move the title blocks at least 60 px inside the right edge, or register them with the auto-framer bbox. Re-check at the end of the push-in.

7. **MAJOR: L2.0/0–1 (USSR map).**
   - **Problem:** the "USSR" is a featureless elongated octagon, more like a blob or sandbar than a country. A painted exhibit-style Lunik sits on top of the map, so the composition reads as a rocket lying on a beach.
   - **Change:** draw a recognisable simplified USSR outline (or a Eurasia map with the USSR shaded), and draw a factory pin with PROBABLE FACTORY + "?" and no location, on 'factory'. Move the Lunik to an inset card at lower left, or link it with a dotted line from the blueprint rather than placing the object on the map.

8. **MAJOR: D3.1/0 (half-empty frame).**
   - **Problem:** for about 2 s the right half of the comic strip is a blank grid while "arrived after the yard closed and" plays. It looks unfinished.
   - **Change:** show both panel frames from the start, with the right one as a dim empty panel (navy, moon outline). Fill it on 'waited'. Alternatively, centre the first panel and slide it left on 'waited'.

9. **MINOR: D6.1 (crate scale continuity).**
   - **Problem:** on the flatcar the "nearly twenty feet" crate is smaller than a boxcar's door section (about a third of a car). In D4.1 it is truck-bed length.
   - **Change:** scale the crate to roughly the length of the flatcar, consistent with D4.1 and D7.0.

10. **MINOR: D2.1 ('and drove').**
    - **Problem:** at 646.2 s the truck is still parked while the narrator says "drove".
    - **Change:** start the truck rolling right on 'drove' (646.42), with headlight beams sweeping, a `truck` sfx, and the new driver stepping back out of frame.

11. **MINOR: L7.0/0–1.**
    - **Problem:** the analyst stands mid-frame holding a giant white ruler in a mostly empty room. The storyboard asks for "at a desk with the BLUEPRINT and a slide rule". The "THE CIA, LATER" tag also overlaps the corkboard photos.
    - **Change:** seat him at the desk with the blueprint rolled out on it and a proper slide rule (two bars + cursor) in hand. Place the tag clear of the corkboard.

12. **MINOR: A1.1/0–1.**
    - **Problem:** Finer stands beside the typewriter, reaching across it. He is not typing.
    - **Change:** seat him behind the typewriter, with keys striking on each word of "NO SIGN OF DISCOVERY" and typewriter sfx per word.

13. **MINOR: A8.0/0–1.**
    - **Problem:** this is a flat night-ocean surface for ~3.7 s, with the "A FEW YEARS LATER" title and nothing moving.
    - **Change:** start the camera descent through the surface on 'extreme' or 'went', not only in A8.1. Add a slow tilt down plus light shafts so the plunge begins in this shot.
