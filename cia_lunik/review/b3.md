# Critic review: b3 (W THE WEAK SPOT, M THE TEAM, G THE GRAB): `web/shots_b3.js`

**Verdict: FIX REQUIRED (not shippable yet).** The heist-planning grammar is strong: the X over the hall, the WEAK SPOT magnifier, the EXPECTED column of "?", the cork plan board with a 7 A.M. deadline clock, the DISPUTED stamp, and the no-escort mirror. Tension builds well into the black card. But there is one invented time, one implied actor, a smartphone-looking prop, several cropped or overlapping labels, the "5"-reads-as-"S" problem again, and a callback (G8) that doesn't actually match the cold open.

Method: contact sheets `contact/critic_b3_01..10.jpg`, plus snaps at 337.2, 339.2, 352.9, 386.2, 416.1, 424.3, 430.5 and 445.0 s, and cold-open snaps at 22.0, 24.8 and 27.5 s (C4/C5, for the callback). Word times come from `voice/lines.json`.

## Required fixes

1. **MAJOR (truth): G15.0/0–1, overlay `nightClockOverlay(c, 19, 0, {sub:'EARLY EVENING'})` (line ~645).**
   - **Problem:** the corner clock reads **7:00 P.M.** The script only says "By early evening". That is an invented precise time, and it sits 30 min before the specialists' 7:30 arrival, so it reads as documented fact.
   - **Change:** show only "EARLY EVENING" (dusk icon, no digits), or a clock with no hands plus the EARLY EVENING sub-label.

2. **MAJOR (truth): G11.0/0, `torchMan` → `P('kolomyakov', …)` (lines 69–70, 569).**
   - **Problem:** the script's hypothetical "If anyone on the Soviet side went looking" is staged as Kolomyakov, the named KGB officer, actually searching with a torch. The tag IF ANYONE LOOKED helps, but a recognisable named character performing the action reads as fact.
   - **Change:** use a generic Soviet silhouette (the `guard` cast member, or an unlit dark figure), dashed or ghosted to read as hypothetical. Keep the tag.

3. **MAJOR (period/craft): M5.1/0–1, flash "READY" meter (line ~344).**
   - **Problem:** the tall rounded dark slab with a glowing green screen and READY reads as a **modern smartphone**, both in the darkroom and next to the camera.
   - **Change:** draw a 1950s flash battery pack or capacitor box: a squat metal box with a round needle gauge sweeping from 0 to FULL, and a small neon "ready" bulb that glows on 'fast'. Keep the yellow fill if you like, but inside a round dial, not a screen.

4. **MAJOR (legibility): G3.2/1 "AROUND 5 P.M." and W1.0 "CHAPTER 5".**
   - **Problem:** the Kalam numeral 5 reads as **S** ("AROUND S P.M.", "CHAPTER S"), the same defect flagged in b5. The step card "5" on the plan board also reads as S.
   - **Change:** for b3's own labels, draw digits with Caveat or write "FIVE P.M.". The chapter-card numeral is in `engine.js` `chapterCard` (Kalam 44px, line ~66). That belongs to the cameraman/orchestrator: switch the number to Caveat or spell it out ("CHAPTER FIVE").

5. **MAJOR (overlap): G3.1/0 "WON'T START" (line ~465).**
   - **Problem:** at 416.1 s the red "WON'T START" is drawn **on top of** the red SOVIET EXHIBITION banner, red on red, and is illegible. The key beat ("so it wouldn't start") doesn't read.
   - **Change:**
     - Move the text to the cab area: a speech-style tag next to the cab at ~(1150, 620), white on a dark plate.
     - Add a visible failure cue: a puff of black exhaust and a shaking cab on 'start', plus a `carengine` sputter sfx cut short.

6. **MAJOR (empty frame): M7.0/0 (385.9–387.0 s, builder-flagged).**
   - **Problem:** the frame is shelves only, and the clerk is cropped at the right edge. For 1 s there is no subject. The camera drifts to the clerk, half out of frame.
   - **Change:**
     - Start with the specialist already at the counter (from M7.0/1) and the clerk fully in frame. Place the clerk at x ≤ 1700 and register him for the framer.
     - Put a blank receipt pad on the counter that starts listing on 'ladders'.
     - Or open on the shop doorway with the bell (`bell` sfx on 'then').

7. **MAJOR (cropping): W2.0/1 chalkboard "AFTER IT CLOSE[D]", M1.2 "FACTORY MARKINGS CE[NTER]", W3.0 left station block, M7.1/0 "[LADD]ERS".**
   - **Problem:** key words are cut by the frame edge. The two worst are "AFTER IT CLOSED", the actual idea of the line, and the FACTORY MARKINGS CENTER plate, which is the named fact.
   - **Change:**
     - W2.0/1: move the chalkboard text to x ≤ 1500, or shrink it to fit the board.
     - M1.2: widen the plate, or reduce the font so the full "FACTORY MARKINGS CENTER" fits at least 40 px inside the plate.
     - W3.0: either show the whole hall/station block or remove the sliver.
     - M7.1/0: keep the item labels at least 60 px inside the frame at the end of the push-in.

8. **MAJOR (callback continuity): G8.0 and G8.1 vs. cold open C4.0 and C5.0 (shots_b1.js).**
   - **Problem:** the storyboard calls for G8–G9 to replay C4–C5 from a new angle, but:
     - **G8.0's map** is a square city-block grid with a horizontal road and a red hook arrow. **C4.0's map** is a navy blueprint with one diagonal road, a branching spur and the label "the last turn-off". They look like two different places.
     - **G8.1/1's canvas crew** is NEW DRIVER (flat cap) + DEAN. **C5.0's crew** is ZAMBERNARDI (moustache, trench) + DEAN.
   - **Change:**
     - Redraw G8.0 as the same diagonal road and spur as C4.0, rotated or zoomed from another angle, with the same label hand ("the last turn-off") and the same RAIL YARD block. Then add one new element, the truck icon arriving.
     - In G8.1 use the same two men as C5.0. The new driver only appears in G9.0, as in C5.1.

9. **MINOR: M2.0/1, the magnifier covers "SERIAL NUMBERS" ("SE?IAL NUMB?RS").** End the lens glide beside the label, or put the label above the plate.

10. **MINOR: M5.0, the "ZAMBERNARDI · CASE OFFICER" tag overlaps the clipboard's left edge.** Move the tag left above his hat, at x ≤ 860.

11. **MINOR: M7.1/1, the "METRIC WRENCHES" label is hidden under the nail puller and receipt; the receipt floats over the shelving.** Stagger the label heights, and land the receipt on the counter.

12. **MINOR: G1.1, the "HELD BACK" tag overlaps the banner's left end.** Hang it on the truck instead, at ~(1450, 840).

13. **MINOR: G9.0/0 (mid-shot), the driver and escort stand in front of the cab, hiding the change of driver.** At 445 s it resolves (new driver in the cab, fine). Have the two men step aside to the right before 'wheel' (443.44 s) so the cab is clear when the line lands.

14. **MINOR: G4.0/1, the dossier is cropped at the top by the push-in (the NAME row is cut at 424.3 s).** Reduce the end zoom, or shift the focus down ~60 px.

15. **MINOR (variety): G10.0/0 → G10.0/1 is the same bare clock, just cropped tighter.** Give /1 a different idea: the second hand sweeping, plus a "BORROWED TIME" label with a sand timer. Or rack to the empty road at the rail yard.

16. **MINOR (palette, cross-file):**
   - **Problem:** the dusk/night skies in M1.1 (airport), G1.1 (exhibition loading) and G5.0 (nightStreet) grade into a mauve/plum band at the top. PRODUCTION.md says never violet.
   - **Change:** these come partly from `SET.nightStreet`/`NIGHT_SKY` in sets.js (visual director) and partly from M1.1's own dusk gradient. Shift the top stop toward navy/teal (#1d2d44) and keep the warm band orange, not pink.

17. **MINOR: G6.0/1, "ANY ESCORT?" sits across the city lights, and an orange slab (the truck's tail) is cut by the bottom edge.** Either show the truck fully in the mirror view, or drop the slab. Set the question above the mirror, or in the dark sky band.

18. **MINOR: G7.0 "no escort." is lowercase in a different hand from every other caption.** Match the caption style ("NO ESCORT."), or make it the narrator's quiet beat as the only lowercase on purpose, but then do the same for all such beats.
